title: Hierarchically Coupled Gaussian Bayesian Networks
section: papers
abstract: Gaussian Bayesian networks are probabilistic graphical models for learning
  network structures from continuous data. Standard approaches typically assume that
  all observations arise from a homogeneous population. In many applications, however,
  the data originate from multiple related but heterogeneous groups that share a common
  network structure while having group-specific network parameters. Pooling all observations
  and inferring a single set of parameters ignores between-group heterogeneity and
  may lead to biased inference. Conversely, estimating group-specific parameters independently
  substantially increases model complexity and inferential uncertainty. We introduce
  a hierarchical prior that enables information sharing across groups. The proposed
  prior preserves group-specific parameters while introducing shrinkage by coupling
  the group-specific mean vectors and covariance structures through shared global
  hyperparameters. The corresponding coupling strengths are inferred from the data,
  allowing the degree of information sharing to adapt automatically to the observed
  heterogeneity across groups. We develop a Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithm
  for posterior inference and demonstrate empirically that the proposed approach improves
  both network structure learning and predictive performance.
