title: Fast Constraint-Based Structure Learning for Continuous-Time Bayesian Networks
section: papers
abstract: Continuous-time Bayesian networks (CTBNs) are a class of probabilistic graphical
  models representing complex processes evolving over time. Applying Bayesian networks’
  state-space factorisation to the time-space mathematical structure of continuous-time
  Markov processes (CTMPs) significantly reduces the number of parameters required
  to model them. However, learning the structure of a CTBN that correctly factorises
  the corresponding CTMP is difficult, as the set of candidate graphs grows exponentially
  with the size of the CTMP random vector. In this work, we present HITON-CTPC, a
  novel constraint-based algorithm to navigate the search space of CTBNs more efficiently.
  Specifically, we incorporate the key steps of the HITON-PC BN structure-learning
  algorithm into the CTPC CTBN algorithm, thereby enabling us to add heuristics for
  determining the order of conditional independence tests. We assessed the performance
  of the proposed solution through an extensive set of synthetic experiments spanning
  a wide range of state-space and time-space configurations. We observed that HITON-CTPC
  achieves competitive structural accuracy while significantly reducing execution
  time. Furthermore, we evaluated the new HITON-CTPC algorithm using the Beijing Multi-Site
  Air Quality data set (Chen, 2017) to assess its ability to reconstruct significant
  relationships in a real-world scenario.
