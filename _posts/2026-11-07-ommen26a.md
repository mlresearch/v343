abstract: 'A linear structural equation model can represent causal relations and latent
  confounders between its variables by means of directed and bidirected edges. The
  set of distributions in such a graphical model can be difficult to describe: conditional
  independence constraints do not suffice in this case, so more general equality constraints
  are needed. We present an algorithm that can check whether two linear structural
  equation models differ by an equality constraint. Using this algorithm, we empirically
  study the partially ordered set of these models, for different subclasses of mixed
  graphs. We show that for some of these classes, a natural generalization of Meek’s
  conjecture does not hold. We also investigate what set of constraints is needed
  to distinguish the models for various classes of graphs.'
