title: Predicting the Risk of Vehicle–Cyclist Crashes in Urban Intersections Using
  Bayesian Networks
section: papers
abstract: The i4Driving European Union project aims to establish a credible and realistic
  human road safety baseline for virtual assessment of Connected, Cooperative, and
  Automated Mobility (CCAM) systems. As part of these efforts, multiple driving simulator
  experiments have been conducted to collect data to verify the developed human driver
  models. This paper focuses on the analysis of data from two urban road experiments
  in the same scenario involving a vehicle and a bicycle. The paper describes the
  development of a knowledge-driven Bayesian network (BN) to predict the risk of a
  right-turn maneuver involving a bicycle traveling straight through a four-way urban
  traffic intersection. The paper also reports on the results of an experimental analysis
  on data from the two urban road experiments, each involving several hundred driving
  sequences with a population of different human drivers. The predictive accuracy
  of the knowledge-driven model is high with a significant prediction horizon. That
  is, in most cases, the model can predict a high-risk scenario with high certainty
  in time to warn the driver, allowing a potential collision to be avoided.
