title: Selective Indicator Elimination in Arithmetic Circuits for Bipartite Noisy-OR
  Bayesian Networks
section: papers
abstract: 'Bipartite noisy-OR Bayesian networks (BN2Os) have been compiled into arithmetic
  circuits (ACs) for exact inference, where evaluation traditionally consists of assigning
  indicator values at the circuit leaves and propagating values upward to the root.
  We introduce selective indicator elimination (SIE), a transformation for compiled
  BN2O ACs that removes indicator-controlled single-variable subcircuits and replaces
  them with cached values. The resulting method has two phases: an offline transformation
  phase that prunes such subcircuits and precomputes their replacement values, and
  an online evaluation phase that parses a query, assigns the appropriate cached values,
  and propagates over the transformed AC. Unlike prior AC compression methods that
  simplify static numeric computation by compressing arithmetic subcircuits consisting
  of multiplication and addition nodes, our approach is the first to eliminate indicator
  leaves together with their associated indicator-controlled computation while preserving
  support for general exact inference queries. We formally prove that SIE preserves
  exact inference correctness. Statistically significant improvements in both circuit
  size and exact inference runtime are empirically observed for $58%$ of the tested
  synthetic BN2Os.'
