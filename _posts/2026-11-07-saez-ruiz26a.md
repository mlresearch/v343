title: Parameter Sensitivity Analysis in Mixtures of Polynomials
section: papers
abstract: Parameter sensitivity analysis examines how a model is affected when some
  of its parameters are changed. In this study, we consider Mixtures of Polynomial
  (MoP) models to represent the probability distributions of continuous variables
  in Bayesian networks (BN). The objective of this study is to analyse the difference
  between a MoP density function and its perturbed versions, and how these perturbations
  propagate to other variables in the BN. A sensitivity measure, which is based on
  $L^1$ distance, is defined to compare the original distribution and the obtained
  distribution after perturbation. The theoretical results indicate that the sensitivity
  measure of a marginal distribution is at most the same as the sensitivity measure
  of the perturbed density. Overall, perturbations in local distributions of MoP Bayesian
  networks propagate to marginal distributions with decreasing intensity —meaning
  that their effect on marginal distributions of descendant variables becomes progressively
  weaker— which suggests that sensitivity analysis should primarily focus on local
  components.
