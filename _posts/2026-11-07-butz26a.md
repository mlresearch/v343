title: Safe Inverse Marginalization in Bayesian Networks
section: papers
abstract: Safe inverse marginalization (SIM) was recently proposed for augmenting
  a Bayesian network with new discrete variables such that their marginalization exactly
  recovers the original network, allowing re-certification to focus exclusively on
  the new variables. Graph-safe marginalization (GSM) was introduced to describe when
  marginalizing the new variable yields a distribution that factorizes according to
  the original DAG, with constraint-optimization methods proposed for constructing
  new conditional probability tables (CPTs). In this paper, we first show that arc-reversal
  without fill-in (AR-NFI) strictly subsumes GSM as a sufficient graphical condition
  for SIM. We then propose constructing new CPTs via unity-potential expansion (UPE)
  rather than optimization. AR-NFI combined with UPE provides a concrete structural
  route to SIM. More fundamentally, UPE recovers the original Bayesian network exactly
  by reconstructing its factorization, from which the original DAG is uniquely identified.
  Hence, UPE alone guarantees SIM, establishing that neither AR-NFI nor GSM is necessary.
  Taken together, these results show that SIM is fundamentally a parametric property,
  with structural conditions characterizing only when recovery can be realized via
  structure-preserving transformations.
