title: A Bayesian Network Approach to Earth Fault Localization in Medium Voltage Electricity
  Grids
section: papers
abstract: Earth faults are the most commonly occurring fault type in Medium Voltage
  electricity distribution grids. Electricity distribution grids in Central Europe
  are compensated, meaning the network continues to operate after an earth fault.
  However, during the time-consuming search process via grid switching, secondary
  faults can occur, leading to outages. Digital twins, through their automated physics-informed
  topology and measurement-integration capabilities, can leverage generated training
  data and available measurements to pinpoint earth-fault locations without additional
  hardware to be deployed in the grid. This paper demonstrates how Bayesian networks
  can aid earth fault localization within a digital twin framework in electricity
  distribution grids. The proposed method uses stored voltage signatures from the
  digital twin to train a Bayesian network model. The results of an experimental analysis
  prove the efficacy of Bayesian networks when applied to observations of EMT voltages
  of the faulty phase. Ongoing work focuses on integrating this approach into a digital
  twin framework to actively support distribution system operators in locating earth
  faults.
