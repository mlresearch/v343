title: Optimized Priority Scheduling for Faster Scalable Belief Propagation

abstract: 'Belief propagation (BP) is a marginalization method using local message
  passing on probabilistic graphical models, seen in disciplines like Bayesian inference,
  statistical physics, and wireless communications. With the proliferation of manycore
  processors with 100s of cores, BP parallelization solves larger marginalization
  problems in reasonable time. On one hand, the classic synchronous algorithm easily
  maps to multithreaded execution with abundant parallelism per synchronization barrier.
  On the other hand, asynchronous algorithms converge on more graphs and reduce total
  work and sequential time to convergence, but are more difficult to parallelize due
  to priority-ordered scheduling. Recently proposed <em>relaxed</em> ordered parallel
  algorithms are plagued by the overheads of a decade-old scheduler. This paper unifies
  recent innovations in concurrent priority schedulers with relaxed parallel BP, using
  the Stealing MultiQueue (SMQ) with lazy priority updates. Prior work proposes different
  relaxed BP algorithms but fails to identify one BP algorithm that outperforms alternatives
  across inputs. We are the first to empirically show relaxed residual BP (RRBP) consistently
  outperforming all other baseline BP algorithms: at 384 threads our SMQ-based RRBP
  outperforms synchronous and other asynchronous BP algorithms by geomean 2.66$\times$
  and $>$1.6$\times$, respectively. Our code is available at github.com/mcj-group/rbp.'
