title: Bayesian Network MPE via LLM-Compiled Semantic Maximizing Circuits
section: papers
abstract: Recent research has shown that Large Language Models (LLMs) are able to
  construct the Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) of a Bayesian Network (BN) at an accuracy
  level similar to the data-driven methods. A natural question that arises is whether
  LLMs can perform emph{logical inference} over these assembled graphical structures
  without having access to associated Conditional Probability Tables (CPTs). We tackle
  this question by focusing on Most Probable Explanation (MPE) inference, a task that
  closely aligns with LLM reasoning. We introduce a novel CPT-free emph{sequential
  semantic inference} method which is structured as a two-phase framework. An offline
  emph{semantic compilation} phase constructs a reusable emph{semantic maximizing
  circuit} by querying the LLM over assignment contexts induced by the DAG and a variable
  elimination ordering. An online emph{evaluation} phase applies evidence through
  circuit labels and resolves MPE queries by traversal, without making calls to the
  LLM at runtime. An empirical study shows that MPE accuracy surpasses 80% across
  six BNs and is even close to the levels reported in the literature for DAG learning,
  thereby demonstrating the potential of LLMs to be used as logical, parameter-free
  inference engines.
