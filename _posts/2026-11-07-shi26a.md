title: Non-Symmetric Information Operators in GMRFs for PDE Inference
section: papers
abstract: Probabilistic approaches to partial differential equations (PDEs) cast the
  solution as a Bayesian inference problem, in which the unknown field is inferred
  from a prior distribution and a likelihood derived from the governing equations.
  When the prior is Gaussian-Markov, the solution admits a Gaussian Markov Random
  Field (GMRF) representation with a sparse precision matrix, enabling efficient inference
  and marginal uncertainty estimates. In this framework, physical constraints enter
  through information operators that define the likelihood. Standard formulations
  rely on symmetric operators, which encode a non-directional flow of information.
  In advection-dominated regimes, this mismatch produces spurious oscillations and
  unreliable uncertainty estimates. In this work we introduce a non-symmetric information
  operator that embeds a physically motivated directional preference into the likelihood.
  Although the operator is non-symmetric, the resulting posterior precision matrix
  remains symmetric and positive definite, preserving full compatibility with standard
  sparse GMRF inference. We evaluate the method on the viscous Burgers’ equation across
  diffusion- to advection-dominated regimes. The non-symmetric operator suppresses
  spurious oscillations and matches finite-element accuracy. Its posterior uncertainty
  remains reliable in advection-dominated regimes where the symmetric operator becomes
  overconfident.
