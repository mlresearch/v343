title: Bayesian estimation of causal effects in Bayesian networks with local structure
section: papers
abstract: We consider the problem of estimating causal effects from observational
  categorical data when the data-generating process can be modeled by an unknown causal
  Bayesian network with local dependence structure in its conditional distributions.
  Capturing local structure in a categorical Bayesian network may significantly reduce
  the number of parameters and facilitate the discovery of weak dependencies and edge
  orientation, which is crucial for causal inference. We extend the Bayesian IDA (BIDA)
  framework to Bayesian networks with local structure, focusing on a specific class
  of local independencies called context-specific independencies (CSI). To this end,
  we build upon previous work that combines Markov chain Monte Carlo methods over
  DAGs with deterministic greedy optimisation of CSI-trees to learn the full network
  structure, and propose a local approach for efficient estimation of pairwise causal
  effects in a given structure, where we apply the same greedy optimisation procedure
  to infer the conditional distributions involved in the backdoor formula. We evaluate
  the proposed method in a simulation study in which we simulate data from synthetic
  Bayesian networks with local structure, as well as a set of benchmark networks.
